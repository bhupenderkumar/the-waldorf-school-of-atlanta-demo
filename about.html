Our Mission & History

The Waldorf School of Atlanta was founded in 1999 by a group of parents dedicated to providing their children with a holistic, arts-integrated education. Our school has since grown to serve over 180 students from diverse backgrounds.

Our teaching approach is inspired by the philosophy of Rudolf Steiner, emphasizing the interconnectedness of art, science, and imagination. Our experienced teachers create engaging, project-based lessons that ignite curiosity and foster a love of learning.

At The Waldorf School of Atlanta, we believe that education is not just about academics, but also about developing the whole child. We offer a range of extracurricular activities, sports, and artistic pursuits that help students cultivate their passions, build friendships, and develop essential life skills.
