Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

In our Pre-K program, students engage in play-based learning, exploring the world around them through art, music, and movement. Our experienced teachers nurture social, emotional, and cognitive growth in a supportive, inclusive environment.

📚

Elementary

In our Elementary program, students delve into a rigorous, arts-infused curriculum that sparks curiosity and creativity. Our small class sizes ensure personalized attention and support as students grow and thrive.

🏫

Middle/Upper

In our Middle and Upper School, students build on their foundational skills, exploring advanced subjects like math, science, and language arts. Our experienced teachers mentor students as they develop critical thinking, problem-solving, and leadership skills.

🔬

STEM

Our STEM program at The Waldorf School of Atlanta offers a unique blend of hands-on learning and innovative technology. Students explore coding, robotics, and engineering, developing essential skills for the 21st century.

🎭

Arts

Our Arts program at The Waldorf School of Atlanta is a vibrant expression of creativity and self-expression. Students engage in music, dance, theater, and visual arts, developing their unique voice and artistic vision.

🏆

Athletics

Our Athletics program at The Waldorf School of Atlanta emphasizes teamwork, sportsmanship, and physical well-being. Students participate in a range of sports, from team sports to individual activities, building lifelong friendships and skills.
